Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using ScarPure MD sheets.

When can I start using silicone sheets after surgery or injury?

You can begin using the sheets as soon as the wound is fully closed and stitches or staples have been removed (usually 1-2 weeks post-op), with your doctor's approval.

How long before I see visible results?

Most users notice significant softening and texture improvement within 4 to 8 weeks, while older scars may take 3 to 4 months of consistent daily wear.

Are these sheets waterproof?

Yes, they are water-resistant. However, removing them briefly during showering or swimming and reapplying afterward ensures maximum adhesion longevity.
